## Answers to Every Question She Will Ask

---

**Q: Who is the target customer?**
> "Three groups. First — senior professionals who make high-value introductions: investors, founders, senior operators, recruiters. Second — marketing agencies running referral campaigns for clients who need proof of performance. Third — community builders and connectors who want to build a verified reputation over time."

---

**Q: How do you make money?**
> "Freemium model. Free tier for individual users — unlimited introductions, basic passport. Pro tier for power users — priority queue, analytics, custom domains. Business tier for agencies and enterprises — white-label, API access, team dashboards, bulk campaigns. We also take a small percentage on verified referral fees when monetary terms are attached."

---

**Q: What makes this different from LinkedIn?**
> "LinkedIn shows you who someone knows. Trueferral shows you what actually happened when they made an introduction. LinkedIn is a directory. Trueferral is a track record. The key difference — LinkedIn lets anyone claim anything. Trueferral only shows verified outcomes. One is reputation by assertion. The other is reputation by proof."

---

**Q: What makes this different from existing referral software like ReferralHero or Referral Rock?**
> "Those tools track clicks and signups for marketing campaigns. Trueferral tracks high-stakes professional introductions — hiring, investment, partnerships. It's not about discount codes and affiliate links. It's about protecting the reputation of senior professionals making introductions that involve real money and real relationships. Different market, different trust level, different price point."

---

**Q: What if someone uses this for fraud?**
> "The system has built-in fraud detection. Self-referrals are blocked. The target must independently confirm — you can't confirm your own introductions. Suspicious patterns are flagged automatically. The hash chain means any attempt to alter records is mathematically detectable. And the Action Queue creates time pressure that exposes lazy or fake introductions."

---

**Q: Is the data safe?**
> "Yes. All data is encrypted in transit with TLS 1.3. The database is on Render's managed PostgreSQL with automatic backups. Sensitive fields like authorization tokens are automatically redacted from logs. The architecture follows clean separation — no raw errors are exposed to users, all error messages are safe client-facing strings."

---

**Q: How long does it take to get started?**
> "Under 2 minutes. Create account, make your first introduction, send the confirmation link. The target needs zero setup — they just click a link in their email. No app download. No account creation on their end."

---

**Q: Can this work for my clients' referral programs?**
> "Absolutely. For a marketing company this is the missing piece. You run the campaign, generate the referrals, but you've never had proof that the referrals actually converted. Trueferral gives you that proof. Every introduction is timestamped, confirmed, and outcome-verified. You can show your client: here are the 47 introductions we generated this month, here are the 23 that confirmed, here are the 12 that converted to deals. That's a report that justifies your fee."

---

**Q: What's the video call feature?**
> "When an introduction leads to a meeting, the meeting can be scheduled directly inside Trueferral. The call is linked to the introduction record — so the full journey from intro to meeting to outcome is captured in one place. Availability scheduling, conflict detection, ratings after the call — all built in."

---

**Q: What does it cost to run?**
> "Currently running on free tier infrastructure — backend on Render, frontend on Vercel, PostgreSQL database. Monthly cost at this scale is zero. As we scale to paid tiers, infrastructure cost is approximately $50-200/month for up to 10,000 active users. Margins are extremely high."

---

**Q: Is this built or is it a prototype?**
> "It's built and live. You're looking at a production deployment. The backend API is running, the database has real tables, every endpoint is functional. This is not a mockup or a prototype. You can create a real introduction right now and it will be permanently recorded in the database."
